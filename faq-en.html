01 · ProductAbout the Product

Q1What is The Buddy patch?
+

The Buddy is a transdermal supplement patch — it delivers nutrients through the skin directly into the body, working within 30 minutes to 1 hour.

Unlike regular pills, it bypasses the stomach — reducing nutrient loss so your body absorbs more, feels the effect faster, and it doesn't upset your digestion.

Q2How many formulas are there? How do they differ?
+

There are currently 3 core formulas, each designed for a different moment in life:

  • Relax Mode — sleep patch · GABA + L-Theanine + Ashwagandha · great for trouble sleeping, jet lag and short sleep.
  • Focus Mode — focus patch · Vitamin B Complex + Zinc + L-Carnitine + Ginkgo Biloba · great for long work days instead of relying on coffee.
  • Night Out Mode — recovery patch · Green Tea + Milk Thistle + NAC + Vitamin B Complex · great before and after a night out.
Q3How is it different from regular pills?
+
  • Faster — effects in 30 minutes (pills take 1–2 hrs).
  • Better absorption — bypasses the stomach and first-pass liver metabolism.
  • No stomach irritation — ideal if pills make you nauseous.
  • Long-lasting 4–8 hrs — releases nutrients gradually.
  • Convenient — just stick it and forget, no need to take with every meal.
Q4Who is it for?
+

Great for: anyone who wants a convenient, fast supplement, dislikes swallowing pills, busy professionals, travellers, students, short sleepers and frequent party-goers.

Consult a doctor before use if you are:

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Managing a chronic condition or taking regular medication
  • Under 18 years old
  • Allergic to any of the ingredients
Q5How big is the patch? Is it visible on skin?
+

The patch is about 2.5 × 2.5 cm, in a translucent off-white tone designed to blend in and stay discreet on the skin.

At just 0.3 mm thick you barely feel it — like a tiny sticker. You can hide it under clothing, hair or a discreet spot.

02 · How to useHow to Use

Q6How do I use it? The correct way to apply
+

Just 3 easy steps:

  • 1) Clean the skin — wipe the area dry and free of cream, oil or sweat.
  • 2) Peel and stick — recommended spots: inner wrist, upper arm, behind the ear, back of the neck, below the chest.
  • 3) Press firmly for 10 seconds — so the adhesive sets. Wear for 4–8 hours, then peel off when done.

Tip Apply 30 minutes before your activity so it kicks in on time.

Q7How soon does it work? How long does it last?
+

Onset: 30 minutes – 1 hour after applying.

Duration: 4–8 hours, depending on the formula and the individual.

  • Relax Mode — works in 30 min · supports deep sleep for 6–8 hrs.
  • Focus Mode — works in 30–45 min · focus for 4–6 hrs.
  • Night Out Mode — works in 30–60 min · lasts 6–8 hrs.
Q8How many patches per day? Can I use multiple formulas together?
+

We recommend 1 patch per formula per day for safety.

You can use 2 formulas in one day (e.g. Focus by day + Relax at night), but leave at least 4 hours between them. We don't recommend more than 2 formulas in a single day.

Q9Can I use it while showering / exercising / sweating?
+

Normal sweat: the adhesive resists light sweat, so it's fine during work or travel.

Exercise / shower: best to remove it first, or apply somewhere less exposed to water (e.g. upper arm instead of wrist).

Note Not waterproof for soaking or swimming — heavy water may loosen the adhesive.

Q10Where should I apply it? Where should I avoid?
+

Recommended areas (smooth, thin, hair-free skin):

  • Inner wrist · inner upper arm · shoulder
  • Behind the ear · back of the neck · nape
  • Below the chest · side of the abdomen

Avoid: skin with wounds, acne, rashes, sunburn or irritation · avoid the face, around the eyes and mucous membranes.

03 · SafetyIngredients & Safety

Q11What are the ingredients? Are they natural?
+

Every formula uses 100% natural extracts, strictly selected — no stimulants, no addictive substances, no prescription drugs.

  • Relax: GABA, L-Theanine, Ashwagandha, 5-HTP, Valerian Root, Marigold Extract
  • Focus: Vitamin B Complex, Zinc, L-Carnitine, Ginkgo Biloba, Centella Asiatica Extract
  • Night Out: Green Tea, Milk Thistle, NAC, Vitamin B Complex, Vernonia Cinerea, Thunbergia Laurifolia
Q12Will I react to the adhesive? Is it irritating?
+

We use medical-grade adhesive — the same standard used in medical settings, safe on skin and dermatologically tested.

People with sensitive skin may see slight redness or itching — if so, remove it immediately and rinse with water. If it doesn't improve within 24 hours, see a doctor.

Tip Patch-test on your wrist before first full use to check for allergies.

Q13Can pregnant / breastfeeding women / children use it?
+

Not recommended for:

  • Women who are pregnant or trying to conceive
  • Breastfeeding women
  • Children and teens under 18

There isn't enough safety data for these groups · always consult a doctor before use.

Q14Can I take herbs / vitamins / other medication alongside the patch?
+

Generally yes, you can combine it with other supplements, since The Buddy is a supplement in patch form, not a drug.

But if you take regular medication (e.g. sleeping pills, blood-pressure, antidepressants, anti-anxiety drugs), consult a doctor first, as some ingredients (e.g. GABA, 5-HTP) may interact.
